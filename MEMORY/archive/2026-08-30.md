## 18:00 — The slice order was inverted, and the epic's own bug was in its sequencing

The epic was sliced 557 → 558 → 559 → 560, cutover last. **That ordering recreates the exact
collision the epic exists to remove.** While the numbering cycle and the counter are both live they
are two allocators: the counter seeds at 79 and issues `D-080`; the cycle computes `max + 1` and
assigns `D-080` to the first inbox file. Flipping the authoring instructions (559) before retiring
the cycle (560) would have produced two decisions under one number.

**A migration's slice order is part of its correctness, not just its convenience.** Whenever an
epic replaces an allocator, the old one dies before anything is pointed at the new one.

## 18:00 — D-079 was defeated by the very doc that describes the deploy

Deploying the cutover by following `ops/agent-worker/README.md` produced a worker with
`build_sha = ""`. The documented `docker build` omits `--build-arg BUILD_SHA=...`, the Dockerfile
defaults the ARG to empty, and the readout goes blank — while the `sha` column keeps tracking `main`
and looks reassuringly current. That is precisely the confusion D-079 exists to prevent.
`robot-refresh` had passed the flag all along; the README never did.

**A runbook nobody actually follows drifts silently, and it fails at exactly the moment someone new
(or an agent) uses it.** Fixed in #377, with a verification step and a note that `robot-refresh`
builds to `$PD_IMAGE` while the compose runs its own `image:` — they agree today, and if they ever
diverge a rebuild succeeds and changes nothing.

## 18:00 — Concurrency tests that cannot fail

The endpoint's "twenty concurrent callers get twenty ids" specs pass against a deliberately broken
read-then-write implementation — **better-sqlite3 is synchronous, so nothing can interleave
in-process.** Verified by mutating the store, not by reasoning about it.

Kept the atomic `UPDATE … RETURNING` (it is right, and cross-process it matters once the worker also
allocates) but rewrote the tests to claim only what they prove. **Mutate the implementation before
believing a concurrency test.**
