## PD-467 — the fix that matters is the event type, not the state

Clearing `stuck` → `queued` on queue entry is the obvious half. The non-obvious half: it must be
logged as **`robot_unstick`**, because that event is the loop's retry-budget boundary
(`resetBoundaryForTicket`). Any other event type hands the ticket back to the loop with its
exhausted budget intact — it dispatches once and immediately re-parks at the cap. Same state,
silently useless.

`awaiting-human` is deliberately NOT cleared: it means an unanswered question, and re-dispatching
just makes the Robot ask it again — the exact failure PD-393 documents about Unstick.

Kept `queuedBlockedByAgentState` (the warn log) even though the clear should make it unreachable.
"Should be unreachable" is precisely the claim that goes stale.

## PD-470 — Steve chose a loop-wide hold over the ticket's per-ticket park (D-063)

Asked before building, because the ticket body specified a per-ticket park with a wake time and I
thought that was wrong: a session limit is a property of the **account**, not of whichever ticket
happened to be running when the quota ran out. Parking that ticket blames it for nothing it did, and
every other queued ticket then hits the same wall and collects the same meaningless failed run.

Three things worth remembering from the build:

- **The promotion is killed structurally, not by special case.** Excluding the `session-limit`
  signature from `countable()` means cap, backoff *and* the transient→deterministic promotion all
  skip it by construction. A special case in the promotion rule would have left the other two.
- **A hold and a pause must stay separate.** `dispatch_paused` must never self-resume (PD-320: an
  auth outage that auto-resumes re-burns the board). A session-limit hold must *always* self-resume.
  One flag serving both would have to behave wrongly for one of them.
- **My own test was wrong, not the cap.** The 12h sanity cap on a parsed reset time rejected two
  assertions I'd written; the cap was right (a roll-to-tomorrow read of "resets 1:00am" at 01:45
  means a ~23h wait — that is a mis-parse, not a quota window). Fixed the test to the overnight
  case that actually happens (late night → next morning).
