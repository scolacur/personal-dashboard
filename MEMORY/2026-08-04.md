## Constraint discovered: `nav-utils.ts` must stay pure TS

`apps/web/vitest.config.ts` deliberately runs **without** the SvelteKit plugin ("pure-TS unit
tests"). Importing `widgets.ts` into `nav-utils.ts` therefore dragged `.svelte` files in and broke
the whole web suite. Fix was to split at the right seam: `nav-utils` resolves the route, the layout
does the widget-count lookup. **Anything imported by a `.spec.ts` must not reach a `.svelte` file.**
