# [Refine] Board shows why a ticket closed — "Split into …" / "Superseded by …" / "Duplicate of …"

**What shipped:** A pure helper `apps/web/src/routes/devops/closure-label.ts` (PD-940, web half of
PD-888) mapping a ticket's `closeReason`/`closedInto` to a label + linked successors, wired into
`TicketCard.svelte` (replaces the ambiguous split badge on a reasoned close; a split *child* now
reads "part of split" instead of a bare "split") and into the ticket detail page as a top banner
(accent-led, below the frozen banner, Epics + Tickets). Visual fixtures gained one closed ticket of
each reason (+ successors) and the spec covers them.

**Worth remembering:**
- The board's **Closed** lane is `defaultHidden: true` (task-tracker `+page.svelte`), so the closed
  *parents* don't photograph on the board by default — only the open split *children* (Backlog)
  show the new lineage badge. The detail-page banner is the main surface for the parents.
- `closedInto` is **server-derived on read** (`closedIntoByTicket` in the store), not stored: split
  pulls `split` relations where `src.close_reason='split'`; duplicate pulls outgoing `duplicates`
  relations; superseded reads `refine_superseded` events' `detail.by` = successor displayId. The
  fixture had to reproduce all three shapes, not just set `closeReason`.
- The existing `visual-fixtures.spec.ts` asserts the exact status *set* on the board — adding
  'closed' tickets broke it until I added 'closed' to that set.
- Gotcha that cost a verify cycle: the spec's ticket factory needs the full `AgentTicket` shape
  (incl. `recurInterval`, `agentTurns`, `refineState`, `refineStale`) for the cast to typecheck, and
  I initially forgot to spread `...overrides` into it (lint caught the unused param).
